Client project

A bilingual online shop for Maar Jewelry, with card payments and shipping to 14 countries

Maar Jewelry makes handmade 925 silver jewelry in Sarajevo. IH-Tech designed and built the brand’s online shop and the admin the Maar team uses to run it.

Challenge

Selling handmade jewelry in two languages and two currencies

A jewelry brand selling from Sarajevo serves two audiences at once: customers at home who shop in Bosnian and pay in convertible marks, and customers abroad who read English and pay in euros.

The same pieces also sell on the OREA marketplace, so the shop has to stay in step with that catalog. And the Maar team runs products, prices, and pages without a developer.

Engagement

Six parts of the build

  1. Storefront in two languages

    Every page exists in Bosnian and English with its own URL. Shoppers switch between convertible marks and euros, and the choice stays with them.

  2. Checkout and payments

    Guest checkout with card payments through Monri and cash on delivery. Each order sends a confirmation and an invoice with a PDF attached.

  3. Products with options

    Product pages carry galleries, sale prices, and options such as ring size or an engraving.

  4. Admin for the Maar team

    17 admin screens cover products, categories, orders, customers, content pages, a blog, and shop settings, including the euro exchange rate.

  5. Search visibility

    A sitemap with language alternates, canonical URLs, and structured data for products and categories. In November 2026 IH-Tech applied the fixes from a Search Console audit.

  6. Catalog sync

    A script keeps availability, prices, and photos in step with the brand’s shop on the OREA marketplace.

How an order moves through the shop

  1. Shopper browses in Bosnian or English
  2. Picks BAM or EUR and adds to the cart
  3. Pays by card through Monri or on delivery
  4. Receives a confirmation and a PDF invoice
  5. The Maar team handles the order in the admin

IH-Tech built the first version between 13 and 16 December 2025: catalog, product options, content pages, blog, newsletter, and card payments. Invoices with PDFs followed on 28 December.

A second pass between 19 October and 3 November 2026 added cash on delivery, product descriptions in both languages, the catalog sync, and the search fixes. The shop runs on Next.js 16 and PostgreSQL, with images served as AVIF and WebP.
