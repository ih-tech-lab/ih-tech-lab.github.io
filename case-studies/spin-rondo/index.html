Spin Rondo, a random picker in three languages with cryptographic draws and live sessions

Spin Rondo is a free random picker from IH-Tech’s ToolForge portfolio. A wheel or a reel draws names, numbers, letters, or images, and every draw uses the browser’s cryptographic random generator.

Challenge

A picker people can trust when they are watching

A random picker gets used at the moments people watch closely: a giveaway winner, a classroom question, a raffle on a stream.

Spin Rondo states how each draw is made, then adds what a single spin lacks: shared lists, stream overlays, rules for live draws, and a gallery of real uses that people review before search engines see it.

Engagement

Six parts of the build

  1. Draws that state their method

    Every spin takes a value from the browser’s cryptographic random source, gets a draw ID, and can go into a CSV log. A public fairness page explains the method and what it does not promise.

  2. Two machines

    A wheel for up to 100 entries and a reel for lists up to 10,000, with 18 wheel skins, 18 palettes, 8 reel designs, and 8 synthesized sound styles.

  3. Share, embed, present

    Short links without an account, an embed view for an iframe or an OBS browser source, fullscreen, and saved wheels on the device or in an account.

  4. Live sessions with rules

    A host runs a session at a short link with one of 20 rule packages for giveaways, quotas, visibility, and groups. The draw runs on the server, and participants join without an account.

  5. A gallery reviewed by people

    Account holders publish a wheel with the story of how they used it. Each one stays out of search results until a moderator approves it, and limits on publishing and links keep spam out.

  6. Three languages from day one

    English, German, and Spanish with 2,579 messages each and a parity test, plus 32 use-case articles and 7 guides.

How a draw moves through Spin Rondo

  1. Paste a list or import a CSV
  2. Spin: a cryptographic random draw
  3. Share, embed, or log the result
  4. Run a live session with rules
  5. Publish to the gallery after review

Spin Rondo is built on ToolForge, IH-Tech’s monorepo for small web tools. 19 shared packages supply accounts, analytics with consent, SEO, the offline app, rate limiting, and the feedback form, so the product work went into the draw engine, the renderers, and the session rules.

The first public version took shape between 28 July and 16 August 2026: accounts, the gallery, and short links on the first day, live sessions on the third, and the new renderer, the reel, and the sound styles in the first week of August. About 950 unit tests and two end-to-end suites on desktop and mobile browsers run in CI. There are no ads and no paid tier.
