Client project

One app for attendance, prepaid packages, and parent updates at a children’s sports studio

Sportski Studio AS runs sports school and corrective gymnastics programs for children in Sarajevo. IH-Tech built studioas.app, a mobile-first web app for the studio owner, assistant trainers, and parents.

Challenge

Keeping every child’s training balance right

The studio sells prepaid packages of trainings, and each session a child attends uses one. That has to stay right across a weekly schedule, several groups, payments taken at the studio, children who pause, and children who train on credit.

Parents pay for those trainings, so they need to see the balance and hear about schedule changes without calling the studio.

Engagement

Six parts of the build

  1. A Today screen for the owner

    The day’s sessions, a one-tap roll call, new payments, and a list of what needs attention: unfinished roll calls, children with no trainings left, and children under their weekly plan.

  2. Roll call and payments on a phone

    Trainers mark attendance and record payments from the session itself. Assistant trainers see only the sessions assigned to them.

  3. A ledger for every training

    Nobody edits a balance by hand. Each payment and each attendance writes an entry to an append-only ledger, and the balance is the sum. A child can train on credit, and the debt comes off the next package.

  4. Schedule and groups

    A weekly template generates the schedule. A week view, past weeks, and a children by sessions matrix show who trained when. Open sessions can start without a roster.

  5. Parent accounts and notifications

    Parents join by invitation and see their child’s balance and history. Reminders, low-balance and expiry notices, and schedule changes go out by email and web push.

  6. Statistics that close each month

    Revenue by program, new and lost clients, and monthly figures that lock when the month ends.

How a training moves through the app

  1. The owner plans the week from a template
  2. A trainer takes the roll call
  3. The ledger deducts one training
  4. The parent sees the balance and gets a notice
  5. The month closes in the statistics

Before writing code, IH-Tech designed 35 static prototypes of every screen and email by role, and wrote an illustrated user guide for the studio.

The app runs on Next.js 16 and PostgreSQL on Neon in Frankfurt, with invitation-only accounts and rate-limited sign-in. Lint rules keep the business rules free of database and interface code, so the balance, plan, and age rules are tested on their own. 43 test files cover those rules and run attendance, payment, schedule, and notification flows against a real PostgreSQL database in CI. A daily job backs up the database and test-restores each backup.
