How we work

How an engagement actually runs

01

Read the estate

Before a roadmap: the code, the pipelines, the tickets, the delivery reality. Due diligence discipline applied to your own systems, so the plan is grounded in what is actually there.

02

Prove it in days

Agentic discovery against the real domain, not a sandbox. A PoC that touches your data and your constraints, fast enough that being wrong is cheap.

03

Put a gate on it

Evaluation before scale. Agent output gets measured, reviewed by an independent role, and held to a standard — because the failure mode of AI delivery is confident output nobody checked.

04

Hand over and step back

To production, with your team able to run it. Standards, tooling and enablement stay behind. A small agency that becomes load-bearing is a risk to its client, and we would rather be re-hired than depended on.

The gap we close

Vibe-coded to a demo. Now it has to survive production.

Teams get further than ever before hitting the same wall: the prototype works, and nobody can safely change it. No tests, no boundaries, secrets sitting in the client, dependencies nobody chose — and an agent that will cheerfully make all of it worse at speed. That is the moment we are useful.

  1. 01

    Security before launch

    Secrets out of the client, authentication and authorisation that actually hold, input validated at the boundary, and a dependency tree somebody has looked at.

  2. 02

    Made safe to change

    Real module boundaries, typed contracts, dead paths deleted. The difference between a codebase a team can extend and one they are afraid of.

  3. 03

    Tests where they pay

    Coverage on the paths that lose money or data, written failing first so the test is proven to work. Not a percentage on a dashboard.

  4. 04

    Actually operable

    Environments, migrations, observability, a rollback that has been rehearsed, and a bill you can predict.

  5. 05

    AI practice that keeps it clean

    Agent standards, review gates and evaluations, so the next feature built at speed does not quietly undo this work.

We do not throw the codebase away and start again. The fastest path to production is almost always the code you already have, made safe to change.
