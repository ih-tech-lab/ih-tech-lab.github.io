How we build your agent

Four steps, from understanding your process to a team that uses the agent every day.

  1. 1

    Week 1

    Learn your process

    We sit with the people who do the work, map the process step by step and find the systems that hold the information. You get a written plan before anything is built.

  2. 2

    Weeks 2 to 3

    Customize your agent

    We build and configure the agent around your process: your rules, your approvals, your wording, and connections to your databases and the tools your teams use.

  3. 3

    Weeks 3 to 4

    Deploy and test with your team

    We install it inside your infrastructure, or in our cloud if you prefer, and test it with your team on real requests until the results meet the standard you set.

  4. 4

    From week 5

    Onboard your team

    We train your employees and administrators, hand over the controls and stay with you: monthly reviews, updates you approve, and the next process when you are ready.

Your data never leaves your company

Most AI services can only reach other cloud tools. The answers your employees need sit in your customer database, ERP, core banking or data warehouse. Our agents are installed in your own infrastructure, next to those systems, and join them with the tools your teams use in one answer or action.

Questions about working with us

Do we have to replace the tools we use?

No. The agents work with the tools your teams already use, such as Microsoft Teams, Slack, Outlook, Jira, Salesforce or SAP, and add your internal systems to them.

Can we start with one process?

Yes, and we recommend it. Most companies start with one team and one process, see the results within weeks, and then add the next agent on the same setup.

What do you need from our side?

One person who knows the process well, access to a test version of the systems involved, your rules for what the agent may do alone, and a few hours a week from the team during testing.

Can you see our data?

Not when the agent runs inside your infrastructure. The agent and its AI models run on your servers, no external AI service is called, and we have no access to what it processes. In our private cloud, data is encrypted, kept in Europe, access-controlled and every access is recorded.

How long does it take?

Usually four to six weeks inside your infrastructure and two to three weeks in our cloud, from the first conversation to your team using the agent.

How is it priced?

It depends on the agent, the deployment and your volume. After the first conversation you get a written proposal with the setup, a timeline and a price.
