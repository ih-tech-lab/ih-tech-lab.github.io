What it looks like in practice

An employee asks one question. The agent checks the leave balance and company holidays, looks at the team calendar and sends the request to the manager. The manager makes the decision.

EmployeeHow many vacation days do I have left, and can I take 22–26 December?

People AgentYou have 9 days left this year. 22-26 December needs 3 days, because the 24th and 25th are company holidays. Your team calendar shows no conflicts, so I’ve sent the request to your manager.

People AI Agent: questions from business leaders

How quickly will we see results?

Employees ask real questions within the first weeks. Most companies see fewer routine requests to HR, and smoother onboarding, during the first month after going live.

What does it cost?

It depends on the number of employees, the systems it connects to and where it runs. After a first conversation you get a written proposal with the price, so you can compare it with what routine HR work costs you today.

Will it replace our HR team?

No. It takes over routine questions and coordination, so HR spends its time with the people who need a person. Decisions on leave, pay, hiring and every sensitive case stay with your people.

What if it gives a wrong answer?

It answers only from your HR records and policies, shows the policy it used and passes anything it is unsure about to HR. Every answer and action is recorded, so you can review and correct it.

Can we control what it does?

Yes. You decide which questions it answers alone, which requests it prepares and who approves them. Leave, letters and changes always wait for a manager or HR. You can pause it at any time.

Is personal data safe?

Each employee only sees their own records, and you decide what the agent may read, so salary, health and case notes can be closed to it entirely. It can run on your own servers, together with its AI models, so nothing leaves your company and we have no access.

Do we have to change our HR systems?

No. It works with Personio, Greenhouse and your own HR and payroll systems, and employees ask in Microsoft Teams, Slack or WhatsApp.

What happens with sensitive topics?

The agent recognises topics such as complaints, health or conflicts at work, does not answer them, and opens a private case for a person in HR. It cannot read those cases.

What do you need from our team?

One person from HR who knows your processes, access to test versions of the HR systems involved, and a few hours a week from HR and a few managers while we test.

Can we start small?

Yes. Most companies start with one process, such as leave requests or HR questions, see the results, and then add onboarding, letters or hiring.
