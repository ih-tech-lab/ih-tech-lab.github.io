System Guardian AI Agent: questions from business leaders

How quickly will we see results?

Your team sees alerts grouped into incidents within the first weeks. Most companies see fewer interruptions and faster fixes during the first month after going live.

What does it cost?

It depends on how many systems it watches, the tools it connects to and where it runs. After a first conversation you get a written proposal with the price, so you can compare it with what outages and night work cost you today.

Will it replace our engineers?

No. It takes the first look at every alert and runs the routine fixes you approved, so your engineers are woken less often and spend their time on the problems that need them.

What if it points to the wrong cause?

Every finding comes with the alerts, log lines and changes behind it, so an engineer can check it in a minute. Anything outside the steps you approved is only a suggestion, and every action is recorded.

Can we control what it does?

Yes. For each action you decide whether it is never allowed, needs a person first or may run alone within a limit. You can pause it at any time.

Can our server logs and system data stay inside?

Yes. It can run on your own servers, together with its AI models, so logs, access lists and system details stay inside your company, no outside AI service is used and we have no access to them.

Does it replace our monitoring tools?

No. It reads Datadog, Grafana, Sentry and your own server logs, works with PagerDuty, Opsgenie, GitHub, GitLab and Jira, and posts in Slack or Microsoft Teams.

Can it change our systems on its own?

Only the actions you approve in advance, such as restarting a stuck service. Everything else is suggested to an engineer first, and big changes are never made without a person.

What do you need from our team?

One person who knows how incidents are handled today, read access to the tools and logs involved, your existing fix procedures, and a few hours a week from the team while we test.

Can we start small?

Yes. Most companies start with a few important services and let the agent only watch and suggest, then allow routine fixes once the team trusts the results.
