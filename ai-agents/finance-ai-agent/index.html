Finance AI Agent: questions from business leaders

How quickly will we see results?

Your team tests the agent on real invoices and bank lines within the first weeks. Most companies see the share of invoices it matches, and the days saved at month end, during the first close after going live.

What does it cost?

It depends on your invoice volume, the systems it connects to and where it runs. After a first conversation you get a written proposal with the price, so you can compare it with what the work costs you today.

Will it replace our finance staff?

It takes over the typing, matching and chasing, so the same team can handle more invoices and spend its time on suppliers, budgets and analysis. Approvals, exceptions and decisions that need judgement stay with your people.

What if it matches something wrongly?

It works only from your own ledger, orders and documents, follows the tolerances you set and passes anything it is unsure about to a person. Every match and action is logged, so you and your auditors can review it.

Can it make payments?

No payment leaves without the approvals you already require. The agent prepares payment runs and releases only what your rules allow, and you can switch off any action at any time.

Is our financial data safe?

It can run on your own servers, with its AI models, so supplier prices, ledgers and bank data stay inside your company and no outside AI service sees them. We have no access to it, and your data is never used to train AI models.

Do we have to change our accounting system?

No. It works with SAP, Oracle, Odoo, Xero and QuickBooks, plus your own ERP, and with Outlook, Gmail, Microsoft Teams, Slack and Expensify.

Can we control what it does?

Yes. You decide which invoices it may approve alone and up to what amount, the price tolerance it works to, and which journals always need a person. Anything switched off becomes a review task for your team.

What do you need from our team?

One person who knows how payables and the close work today, access to test versions of the systems involved, and a few hours a week from the team while we test.

Can we start small?

Yes. Most companies start with supplier invoices or bank reconciliation for one entity, see the results, and then extend the agent to expenses and the close.
