Knowledge Guide AI Agent: questions from business leaders

How quickly will we see results?

Employees ask real questions within the first weeks. Most companies see fewer repeated questions to HR and IT, and faster answers for new joiners, during the first month after going live.

What does it cost?

It depends on the number of employees, the sources it reads and where it runs. After a first conversation you get a written proposal with the price, so you can compare it with the time your teams spend searching and answering today.

Will it replace our HR or IT staff?

No. It takes over the repeated questions, so HR, IT and your experts spend their time on the work that needs them. Decisions and sensitive topics stay with your people.

Does it make things up?

It only answers from your own documents and always shows where the answer came from. If the answer isn’t documented, it says so and sends the question to the team that owns the topic.

Can we control what it answers?

Yes. You choose its sources, decide which answers are verified and which topics always go to a person, and owners approve every new answer before it is used. You can pause it at any time.

Are our documents safe?

It can run on your own servers, together with its AI models, so your documents stay inside your company and no outside AI service is used. We have no access to them, and your data is never used to train AI models.

Will employees see documents they shouldn’t?

No. Each employee only gets answers from documents they are already allowed to open, following the access rules you already have.

Do we have to move our documents?

No. It reads your intranet, Confluence, SharePoint, file shares and policy library where they are today, and employees ask in Microsoft Teams, Slack or the intranet.

What do you need from our team?

The owners of your main sources, access to test versions of them, and a few hours a week from HR, IT and a handful of employees while we test.

Can we start small?

Yes. Most companies start with one department or one set of documents, such as HR policies, see the results, and then add more sources.
