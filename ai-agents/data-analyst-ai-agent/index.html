Data Analyst AI Agent: questions from business leaders

How quickly will we see results?

Your managers ask real questions within the first weeks. Most companies see fewer requests reaching the data team, and faster answers, during the first month after going live.

What does it cost?

It depends on how many people use it, the data sources it connects to and where it runs. After a first conversation you get a written proposal with the price, so you can compare it with what reporting costs you today.

Will it replace our analysts?

It takes over the repeat questions and routine reports, so your analysts spend their time on forecasting, testing and deeper analysis. Definitions, data quality and the answers that matter most stay with your people.

How do we know the numbers are right?

Every answer shows which data it used and how it was calculated, so analysts can check it. It uses only the definitions your team has agreed, and it warns you when a source is late or incomplete instead of answering from bad data.

Can we control what it does?

Yes. You decide which sources it reads, which reports it may send alone and which need a person’s approval first. You can pause it at any time.

Is our data safe?

It can run on your own servers, with its AI models, so sales, finance and customer data stay inside your company and no outside AI service sees them. We have no access to it, and your data is never used to train AI models.

Who can see what?

Each person only sees the data they are already allowed to see. The agent has no access of its own: it uses the rights of the person asking, and checks that everyone in a shared channel may see the same detail.

Does it need a data warehouse, or new dashboards?

No. It works with your data warehouse, such as Snowflake or BigQuery, or directly with your ERP, databases and spreadsheets. It works alongside Tableau, Looker and Power BI and can point to the right dashboard.

What do you need from our team?

One person who knows your data and how reports are used today, access to test versions of the sources involved, and a few hours a week from the data team while we test.

Can we start small?

Yes. Most companies start with one team or one subject, such as sales or month-end figures, see the results, and then extend the agent to other teams.
