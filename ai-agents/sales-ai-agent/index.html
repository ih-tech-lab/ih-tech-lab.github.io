Sales AI Agent: questions from business leaders

How quickly will we see results?

Your reps test the agent on real meetings and quotes within the first weeks. Most companies see the time saved on meeting preparation, CRM updates and quotes during the first month after going live.

What does it cost?

It depends on the size of your sales team, the systems it connects to and where it runs. After a first conversation you get a written proposal with the price, so you can compare it with the selling time your reps lose to admin today.

Will it replace our salespeople?

No. It takes over the preparation and admin around selling, so reps spend more time with customers. Relationships, negotiations and decisions on price stay with your people.

What if it gets something wrong?

It works only from your own systems, price lists and documents, follows the limits you set and flags anything it is unsure about. Every brief, quote and CRM change is recorded, so you can review and correct it.

Can we control what it does?

Yes. You decide which records it may update, which discount each rep may give and who approves the rest. You can pause it at any time.

Are our customer data and prices safe?

It can run on your own servers, together with its AI models, so customer records, prices and margins stay inside your company and no outside AI service is used. We have no access to them, and your data is never used to train AI models.

Do we have to change our CRM?

No. It works with Salesforce, HubSpot and Zoho, Outlook, Gmail, Microsoft Teams and Slack, and adds your order system, price lists and contracts to them.

Does it email customers on its own?

Only if you allow it. By default it drafts emails and follow-ups, and the rep checks and sends them.

What do you need from our team?

One person who knows how your sales process works today, access to test versions of the CRM and the systems involved, and a few hours a week from a handful of reps while we test.

Can we start small?

Yes. Most companies start with one team or one job, such as meeting briefs or quotes, see the results, and then extend the agent to the rest of sales.
