Document AI Agent: questions from business leaders

How quickly will we see results?

Your team tests the agent on real contracts and invoices within the first weeks. Most companies see shorter review times and fewer documents typed by hand during the first month after going live.

What does it cost?

It depends on how many documents you handle, the systems it connects to and where it runs. After a first conversation you get a written proposal with the price, so you can compare it with what the work costs you today.

Will it replace our lawyers or clerks?

It takes over the searching, comparing and retyping, so the same team can handle more documents and spend its time on negotiations and judgement. Decisions and signatures stay with your people.

What if it reads something wrong?

It links every finding to the clause or field it came from, and fields it is unsure about wait for a person to confirm. Every change it makes is recorded, so you can review and correct it.

Can we control what it does?

Yes. You decide which documents it may read, which details it may post on its own and up to what amount, and what always needs an approver. You can pause it at any time.

Are our contracts and records safe?

It can run on your own servers, together with its AI models, so documents stay inside your company, no outside AI service is used and we have no access to them. It follows the access rights you already have, so employees only see what they are allowed to see.

Do we have to change our document systems?

No. It works with your document archive, SharePoint, shared drives, Outlook, DocuSign and your ERP. Scanned contracts and PDFs become searchable like any other document.

Does it give legal advice?

No. It compares documents with the rules your legal team defines and flags the differences. Your lawyers make the decisions.

What do you need from our team?

One person who knows how documents are handled today, your standard clauses or playbook, access to test versions of the systems involved, and a few hours a week from the team while we test.

Can we start small?

Yes. Most companies start with one document type, such as supplier contracts or invoices, see the results, and then extend the agent to other teams.
