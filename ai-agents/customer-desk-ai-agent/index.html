Customer Desk AI Agent: questions from business leaders

How quickly will we see results?

Your team tests the agent on real requests within the first weeks. Most companies see the share of routine requests it answers, and the time saved, during the first month after going live.

What does it cost?

It depends on your request volume, the systems it connects to and where it runs. After a first conversation you get a written proposal with the price, so you can compare it with what the work costs you today.

Will it replace our support staff?

It takes over the repetitive part of the work, so the same team can handle more requests and spend its time on the customers who need a person. Decisions, exceptions and sensitive conversations stay with your people.

What if it gives a wrong answer?

It answers only from your own systems and documents, follows the limits you set, and passes anything it is unsure about to a person. Every answer and action is recorded, so you can review and correct it.

Can we control what it says and does?

Yes. You decide which questions it answers alone, which actions it may take and up to what amount, and how it should sound. You can pause it at any time.

Is our customer data safe?

It can run on your own servers, where every conversation and customer record stays inside your company and we have no access to it. Your data is never used to train AI models.

Do we have to change our help desk or tools?

No. It works with Zendesk, Intercom, Salesforce, Microsoft Teams, Slack and the other tools your team already uses.

Which channels can customers use?

Chat, email, messaging apps such as WhatsApp, Messenger and Telegram, and text messages. The agent works in writing only.

What do you need from our team?

One person who knows how support works today, access to test versions of the systems involved, and a few hours a week from the team while we test.

Can we start small?

Yes. Most companies start with one channel or one team, see the results, and then extend the agent to the rest of support.
